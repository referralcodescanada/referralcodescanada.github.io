## How long the bonus should take

Under Wealthsimple’s referral terms, both you and your referrer receive **{bonus}** in your Wealthsimple **Chequing** account, usually **within 24 hours** after your first qualifying deposit.

If a day or two has passed since your deposit went through and nothing showed up, go through the checklist below before contacting support.

## The 7-point checklist

- **The referral is actually linked.** Open the gift icon (Rewards) in the app and check the referral status — Wealthsimple explains how in [Confirm your referral]({confirmUrl}). No code? Add **{code}** now.
- **You added the code in time.** A code can be applied up to **7 days after your first deposit**. After that, the app no longer accepts it.
- **You deposited at least {minDeposit}**, net of any withdrawals.
- **The money came from outside Wealthsimple** — a bank account or another financial institution. Transfers from other Wealthsimple clients, Wealthsimple bonuses and internal moves don’t count.
- **The deposit landed within 30 days** of the referral being linked.
- **You have a Wealthsimple Chequing account** in good standing, as the primary owner. The bonus is paid there; without one, it expires after 90 days.
- **You’re a new client**, a legal resident of Canada and the age of majority in your province — and you didn’t refer the person who referred you.

## Added the code too late?

If you’re past the 7-day window, the app won’t let you enter a code anymore. Wealthsimple support can sometimes link the referral manually, but it isn’t guaranteed.

## How to contact Wealthsimple support

Use the help section of the app or the [Wealthsimple Help Centre]({helpUrl}). To get a quick answer, have this ready:

1. The referral code you used (**{code}**).
2. The date you opened your account.
3. The date, amount and source of your first deposit.
4. A screenshot of the referral status in the Rewards section, if you have one.

> Rules summarized from the [official referral terms]({termsUrl}). Wealthsimple can change them at any time.
